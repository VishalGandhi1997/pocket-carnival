Privacy Policy — Khel Mela

Last updated: 9 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Khel Mela ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you play our mini-games on Android. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

The app itself does not ask you to create an account to play. We handle:

  • Game data stored on your device: your coins, best scores, game levels, sound preference, and (if you set one) a display name and avatar. This is stored locally on your device.
  • Optional sign-in: if you choose "Sign in" to sync progress across devices, we receive a basic account identifier from your sign-in provider (e.g. Google) to save your progress. You can use the app fully as a guest without signing in.
  • Advertising & analytics data: collected by our advertising partner (see below) to show and measure ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We show ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process data including your device's advertising ID, approximate location (derived from IP), and device/usage information.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by law, we request your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

How we use information

  • To run the games and save your progress and rewards.
  • To display ads and reward you with in-game coins for watching them.
  • To keep the app secure and prevent abuse/fraud.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that make the app work — principally Google (AdMob for ads, and Firebase if you sign in) — who process it under their own privacy terms.

Children

Khel Mela is a general-audience casual game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices & rights

  • Play as a guest without signing in.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in device settings.
  • Clear all local game data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete all of your data at any time:

  • In the app: open your profile (avatar, top-left) → "Delete my data" → confirm. This erases your coins, scores, levels, skins, and profile from the device (and, once cloud sync is enabled, your associated server records).
  • By request: email support@khelmela.app with the subject "Delete my data" and we will remove any data associated with you within 30 days.

In-app purchases & virtual currency

Coins are a virtual, in-game currency with no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash or prizes. The app contains no real-money gaming, wagering, or gambling.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@khelmela.app.
